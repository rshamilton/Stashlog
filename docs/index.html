Questions

Is Stashlog really free?

Yes. Every feature is free: unlimited rooms, unlimited items and unlimited PDF reports. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads.

Does Stashlog upload my video or photos?

No. Stashlog has no servers. Videos and photos are analyzed on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. Nothing leaves your phone unless you share an export yourself.

Can I make a home inventory by filming a video of each room?

Yes. Film a slow walkthrough. Stashlog samples frames, recognizes items on-device, and suggests an item list with quantities for you to confirm. You stay in control: nothing is added until you tap Add.

Will my insurance company accept the PDF?

The report includes what insurers typically ask for: a room-by-room itemized list, photos, make and model, serial numbers, purchase dates and prices, replacement values, totals and attached receipts. No app can guarantee acceptance, so check with your insurer.

What if my phone is lost in the same fire or flood?

Export your PDF and keep a copy somewhere else: iCloud Drive, email, or a USB drive. iCloud device backup also covers Stashlog's data.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later, including iPhone 11, 12 and 13. Scanning doesn't require Apple Intelligence. On iPhones that have it, label reading gets an extra boost from Apple's on-device model.

Is it open source?

Yes. The full source code is on GitHub under the GPLv3 license, so anyone can check the privacy claims.
