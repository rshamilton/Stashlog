A walkthrough, not a weekend

Most people never finish a home inventory because typing in 300 things is miserable. Stashlog starts the list for you.

  1. Film a roomWalk in, pan slowly, walk out. Or pick photos you already took.
  2. Check the listStashlog suggests what it saw (sofa, TV, lamps ×2, guitar) with a photo of each. Untick anything wrong.
  3. Snap the labelsPhotograph the sticker on the back of the TV or the receipt. Brand, model, serial number, price and date fill in.
  4. Export and store it somewhere safeA PDF and a spreadsheet. Email it to yourself or put it in iCloud Drive, so it survives if the house (and the phone) doesn't.

The report

Laid out the way an adjuster reads it: room by room, with photos, make and model, serial numbers, what you paid, what it costs to replace, and totals. Receipts go in the back.

  • Unlimited rooms, items and reports
  • PDF for your insurer, CSV for your spreadsheet
  • Your name, address and policy number on page one

Your house stays on your phone

A video of every room, with every valuable in it, is about the last thing that should sit on someone else's server. So it doesn't.

  • Recognition runs on the iPhone with Apple's Vision framework
  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads
  • Works offline, and on iPhones without Apple Intelligence
  • Free, with nothing to unlock later
  • Open source (GPLv3), so you don't have to take my word for it

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Unlimited rooms, items and PDF reports. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads.

Does it upload my video or photos?

No. Stashlog has no servers. Video and photos are analyzed on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it unless you share an export yourself.

Will my insurance company accept the PDF?

It includes what insurers usually ask for: an itemized list by room, photos, make and model, serial numbers, purchase dates and prices, replacement values, totals and receipts. No app can promise acceptance, so it's worth asking your insurer.

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later, including iPhone 11, 12 and 13. Scanning doesn't need Apple Intelligence.

Can I see the code?

All of it, on GitHub, under the GPLv3 license.
