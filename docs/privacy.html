Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Short version: Stashlog does not collect any data. The app has no servers, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your rooms, items, photos, receipts, serial numbers and values are stored only on your iPhone. Walkthrough videos are analyzed on your iPhone and then deleted from the app's temporary storage. Stashlog never stores or uploads the video.

On-device processing

Item recognition and text reading use Apple's Vision framework on your device. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Stashlog may use Apple's on-device language model to tidy up text read from labels. Stashlog does not use Apple's Private Cloud Compute or any other cloud model.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you film a walkthrough or take a photo. The system camera records audio with videos. Stashlog never uses or stores that audio.

Backups

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, iOS includes Stashlog's data in your device backup, which Apple manages under your Apple Account. Stashlog itself never sends your data anywhere.

Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share an export (PDF or CSV), for example by email or by saving it to Files. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

Stashlog collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Verify it yourself

Stashlog is open source. You can read every line of code at github.com/rshamilton/Stashlog.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
