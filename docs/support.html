Help

Capturing a room quickly

  • Open a room and tap Capture Items. Fill the frame with one thing and tap the shutter.
  • Don't stop to type. Tap a suggested name, Same as last, or a name you've used before, or leave it and keep shooting.
  • Several of the same thing? Shoot one and tap +1 for each extra.
  • Bad shot? Tap Undo.
  • Items you didn't name are marked "to review" in the room list, so you can tidy up later.

Serial numbers and receipts

Open an item, tap Read from label, and photograph the sticker or nameplate. It's usually on the back or underside. For receipts, add the photo under Receipt and the price and date are filled in when they can be read. Always double-check the values.

Exporting your report

Tap the share button on the main screen to create a PDF and a CSV. Save a copy somewhere other than your phone, such as iCloud Drive, email or a USB drive, so it survives if the phone doesn't.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.
