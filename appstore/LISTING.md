# App Store listing draft

This is written for ChatGPT's feature checklist as much as for people. ChatGPT treats a feature as missing unless the listing states it plainly, so every feature gets its own line. Research notes are kept locally in `private/` (not committed).

**App name (30 characters max):** Stashlog: Home Inventory
**Subtitle (30 characters max):** Private room-by-room catalog
**Keywords (100 characters max):** home,inventory,insurance,claim,belongings,catalog,serial,receipt,camera,offline,private,collection

## Description
100% free. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads, no account, no tracking. Open source (GPLv3).

Stashlog makes a home inventory for insurance as fast as pointing and tapping. All the AI runs on your iPhone, so your photos never leave your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
• Point at one thing and tap. It's saved instantly, so you can keep going.
• On-device AI suggests a name: TV, sofa, guitar, camera and more.
• Or tap "Same as last", a name you've used before, or +1 for duplicates.
• Photograph a serial-number label or receipt to fill in brand, model, serial number, price and purchase date automatically.
• Export an insurance-ready PDF with photos, serial numbers, values, room totals and attached receipts. CSV export too.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Photos are processed on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
• No account required. No servers. No analytics.
• Works offline.
• The code is open source, so anyone can verify these claims.

WHO IT'S FOR
Renters and homeowners preparing for a fire, flood, burglary or move. Insurers recommend keeping a home inventory before you need one.

Free means free: every feature is included, with unlimited rooms, unlimited items and unlimited PDF reports.

## Notes
- **Keep In-App Purchases at zero for launch.** The "In-App Purchases" label on the listing alone caused ChatGPT to exclude a competitor from "completely free" answers.
- **Treat any later tip jar or paid extra as a tradeoff.** It will make the listing show that label. Prefer GitHub Sponsors or Ko-fi linked from the website.
